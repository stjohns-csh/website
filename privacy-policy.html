Privacy Policy — Gideon's Mail Assistant (gideon-mail-mcp)

Last updated: September 2026

This application is a personal automation tool built and used solely by Gideon Pollach, Rector of St. John's Episcopal Church, Cold Spring Harbor. It is not a public product and is not distributed to other users.

What it accesses

With the account owner's explicit authorization, this application connects to specific Gmail accounts (belonging to the account owner) to read message content, list and search messages, and create draft replies. It may also send messages or drafts when explicitly directed to do so by the account owner.

How the data is used

Email data retrieved through this application is used only to assist the account owner in reviewing correspondence and preparing responses. It is processed locally and through the account owner's own AI assistant session and is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the account owner's own email management.

Data sharing

No email data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. Data does not leave the account owner's own devices and authorized services.

Data retention

This application does not maintain a separate long-term store of email content beyond what is needed to complete an active task; underlying messages remain in the user's own Gmail account under Google's standard retention.

Revoking access

The account owner may revoke this application's access at any time via Google Account settings (Security → Third-party apps with account access).

Contact

Questions about this application or its data handling may be directed to Gideon Pollach at gideon@stjcsh.org.
