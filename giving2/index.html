Give

Your generosity sustains everything we do together — worship, music, formation, pastoral care, outreach to neighbours in need, and the care of our church and grounds. Every gift matters.

Give online through REALM

To make or renew an annual pledge, see Make a Pledge. If you are not sure which option best fits your circumstances, we would be glad to talk it through — write to giving@stjcsh.org.

Ways to Give

Pledges & Annual Support
Annual pledge

Annual pledges and ongoing contributions are the foundation of parish life at St. John’s. You may give weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually, and you may adjust your pledge as your circumstances change.

Make a pledge →

Multi-year commitments

Two-, three-, or five-year commitments help St. John’s plan confidently for the future. Write to giving@stjcsh.org for a pledge form.

Designated & Memorial Gifts
Designated gifts

You may direct your gift to the music program, Concerts by the Pond, the Floral Fund, St. Matthias Fellowship, Youth Ministry, Outreach Ministry, the Rector’s Discretionary Fund, buildings and grounds, the cemetery, or the endowment. Please write to us with any questions.

Memorial & tribute gifts

Give in memory or in honour of someone dear to you. We will notify the person or the family you designate, without disclosing the amount.

Matching gifts

Your employer may match your charitable gift. Your human resources office can tell you whether your gift qualifies.

Tax-Wise Giving
Appreciated securities

Gifts of appreciated stock or other securities may carry significant tax benefits. Write to giving@stjcsh.org for transfer instructions.

IRA qualified charitable distributions

If you are 70½ or older, you may make a tax-advantaged gift directly from your IRA. Write to giving@stjcsh.org for details.

Donor-advised funds

You may recommend a grant to St. John’s through your donor-advised fund. Write to giving@stjcsh.org for the details your fund will ask for.

St. John’s cannot give tax advice. Please speak with your own adviser about how any of these gifts would apply to your situation.

Legacy & Named Gifts
Named & endowed gifts

A significant gift can create a lasting legacy at St. John’s. Naming opportunities are available for endowed gifts supporting music, outreach, the cemetery, and other priorities. Write to giving@stjcsh.org to discuss opportunities and recognition.

Planned gifts — The 1835 Society

Remember St. John’s in your will, retirement account, life-insurance policy, charitable trust, or other estate plan, and help sustain the parish for generations to come. Gifts of real estate may also be considered, case by case. Write to giving@stjcsh.org to learn more.
