Spiritual Practices

Spiritual Practices

Nine free prayer practices for the places you already inhabit — a backyard, a golf course, a harbor, a meditation garden. Each one asks only that you stop long enough to receive what is already being offered. Print them, forward them, use them with a group, or walk them alone.

These come from the BlueGreen Theology Project, the Rector’s decade of writing between sermons, beside our own pond — watershed discipleship, creation care, and a theology that finds the church year already written into the seasons outside our doors. Visit the BlueGreen Theology Project →

The Church Year

Practices that follow the seasons the church already keeps, and find their creation parallel. Walk them when the season comes around, or whenever you need them.

Praying with Hildegard

Hildegard of Bingen’s viriditas as a prayer experience. Pray through the greenings, and stand honestly in the dry ground.

Where You Already Are

No season required. These begin from the ground under your feet: a garden, a fairway, a boat, a patch of grass behind the house. The particular place is not an illustration — it is the whole point.
