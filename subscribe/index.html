What you’ll receive

At St. John’s

Our weekly parish email, sent Thursdays. Service times and readings for the coming Sunday, what is happening in the parish, who is on the prayer list, and a word from the clergy.

Sunday Reflections

A short midweek piece of writing from the rector, sent to the same list.

You can read both without subscribing: At St. John’s and Sunday Reflections are posted here as they go out.

We send nothing else, we never share your address, and every email carries an unsubscribe link. If you would rather not use the form, call the office at (516) 692-6368 or email kim@stjcsh.org and we will add you.
