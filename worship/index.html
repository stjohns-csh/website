Worship ministries

Acolytes are young people in 6th grade and above who assist the clergy during the 10:00 a.m. service. Altar Guild members care for the linens and prepare the altar for each service. Lectors, chalice bearers, ushers and greeters all share in leading our common worship, and newcomers are always welcome.

To volunteer, please contact the parish office.
