Benefit to Save St. John’s Pond

Thursday, September 10, 2026
Cocktails 6:00 p.m. · Program & auction 7:00–9:00 p.m.
St. John’s Church, 1670 Route 25A, Cold Spring Harbor

The St. John’s Pond Watershed Alliance — the 501(c)(3) restoring St. John’s Pond here in Cold Spring Harbor — invites you to our second annual Save the Pond Benefit. Join us for an evening of music, festive fare, and a live and silent auction, all to support pond restoration, spillway repair, and other capital projects. Visiting for the first time

Come as you are. There is no dress code, no expectation that you know when to stand or sit, and no obligation in the offering plate. Arrive a few minutes early and someone at the door will give you everything you need and show you where to sit.

Our mission

We invite all to join us at any of our worship services, and it is our hope that you will find a spiritual home here. Our mission priorities as a parish church are compelling and beautiful worship; supporting our families across the life-cycle; reaching out to our neighbours in need; becoming a more connected community; and stewardship of our natural environment.

Life is short and we have not too much time to gladden the hearts of those who are traveling life’s way with us. So be swift to love, and make haste to be kind.
— based on a prayer by Henri-Frederic Amiel
